Nemi

Track your daily steps and see how your movement changes over time.

Nemi reads step totals recorded by your phone through Health Connect and saves
daily totals on your device. Set a daily step goal, browse past weeks, and see
your best day and lifetime steps. Export your history as a CSV backup or import
a previous backup from Settings.

Nemi is open source: https://github.com/sjaramillo10/Nemi
Inspired by Pedometer, originally created by Thomas Hoffmann.
